Skycubed Platformer

This is what I consider as my first standalone project, as it successfully bridged the gap from "Java Beginner" to "Object Oriented Programmer". The Cyv Project

My love for Minecraft truly runs deep, but particularly technical Minecraft parkour and movement physics. When I initially dove into Minecraft Parkour, the client-sided utilities were quite limited. This lead to me first creating MorphMPK as a client-sided mod adding additional, basic utilities to assist in movement and aid in calculation. While primitive and very much outdated, MorphMPK was the precursor for something so much larger.

CyvClient

Jumping from creating a simple 1.8.9 Forge mod to a full-scale Minecraft Client was immense, but six months of continuous effort allowed CyvClient to be born. This client combines the many features of MPKMod with many open-source optimization and utility features beloved by veteran Minecraft players for a slick, streamlined parkour experience.

Ultimately, standalone clients are incredibly hard to sustain, and one for something as focused and narrow as Minecraft Parkour is better as just a mod, for the sake of user simplicity. While I no longer maintain CyvClient, it still sees hundreds of users daily. The JAR download is located here. I unfortunately cannot provide public source code due to Minecraft's Terms of Service.

CyvForge & CyvFabric

CyvForge and CyvFabric are ports of CyvClient's parkour features to the Forge 1.8 and Fabric 1.20+ modloaders respectively. They are effectively modernized versions of MPKMod, with more robust features and many more utilities. These projects are maintained by myself and two other volunteer developers from the Minecraft Parkour community.
